## Body mesh foundation
Official Blender Human Base Meshes bundle 1.4.1, realistic female body, CC0 according to its embedded README. Subdivision and anatomical/pose edits made for this candidate; not an original sculpt or scan.
https://download.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/human-base-meshes-bundle-v1.4.1.zip
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Wings, crown, hair, emblems and staging are modern additions. No claim that this figure exactly reproduces the relief.
